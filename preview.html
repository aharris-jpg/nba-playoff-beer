Methodology: Beer prices were standardized to a 16-ounce serving size. Because official playoff concession pricing is rarely disclosed, projected playoff prices apply a uniform 15% postseason premium to regular-season concession prices to account for increased demand and the removal of regular-season promotional pricing.
Two-beer totals represent one beer for each adult in a family-of-four model.
